Launchpad documentation

The PIXORUN launch lifecycle combines mission tokens, a constant-product bonding curve, atomic Liftoff migration, PIXORUN Swap and protocol fee distribution.

01 · Fixed token supply

Every mission has 1,000,000,000 tokens minted once. 793,100,000 (79.31%) are allocated to the bonding curve. 206,900,000 (20.69%) are reserved for liquidity at Liftoff. Tokens have no mint function, owner privileges, pause, blacklist or transfer tax.

02 · Constant-product bonding curve

Each mission trades against its own constant-product curve using virtual reserves. Price rises on buys and falls on sells. The fuel meter is the amount of curve allocation sold divided by 793,100,000. The testnet reference calibration uses a 0.1 ETH virtual native reserve and reaches Liftoff after roughly 0.28 ETH in net buys. Mainnet calibration must be published before launch.

Buy and sell trades charge 1% of gross native amount. Default slippage is 1%; the trade page currently offers 0.5%, 1%, 2% and 5%. A transaction that exceeds its slippage limit reverts without trading.

03 · Liftoff

When a buy fills the curve, Liftoff occurs in that transaction. If the buy exceeds the remaining curve allocation, the unused amount is refunded. A 3% graduation fee is taken from raised native. The remaining native and matching tokens seed a PIXORUN Swap pool at the curve's final price. Reserved tokens not needed for that price are burned. LP tokens are sent to an irrecoverable dead address, permanently locking liquidity.

04 · Fee distribution

  • Curve buy/sell fee: 1% of gross — 30% PIXORUN team, 20% PIXORUN staking pool, 50% mission creator.
  • PIXORUN Swap fee: 1% of native side — 30% team, 20% stakers, 50% mission creator.
  • Creation fee: fixed fee (testnet reference 0.0001 ETH) — 70% team, 30% stakers.
  • Liftoff fee: 3% of raised native — 70% team, 30% stakers.

Creator shares accrue per mission and can be claimed from Creator. If a staking contract is configured, staker shares are credited from the next block. When the staking address is zero or reward forwarding fails, the staker share is redirected to the team. Team transfers must never block trading; failed team payments remain claimable.

05 · PIXORUN staking

Staking is planned but is not active on Robinhood Chain Testnet. The deployed Fee Distributor has a zero staking address, so the staker share is currently redirected to the team. Do not send tokens to a staking contract until a verified deployment is published.

06 · Token creation and metadata

Name 1–32 UTF-8 bytes; ticker 1–10 English letters or numbers. Optional token logo and banner accept PNG, JPG, WEBP or GIF up to 2 MB each. Description supports up to 1,000 characters, with optional website, X/Twitter and Telegram links. Token name, ticker, creator, token and curve addresses, transaction hash, block number, transaction value and gas details are recorded in the shared PIXORUN registry. Metadata and image checksums are saved after a free signature from the launching wallet. Images are stored in PIXORUN Supabase Storage and the same token details appear on token, Explore, Profile, Creator Studio and homepage listings. Testnet creation fee reference is 0.0001 ETH.
